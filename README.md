# Introduction
<b>SEE-ME-AGAIN</b> is currently in the development phase. Any issue inside the engine is currently being addressed. The game-engine was chosen to be Unity, due to the incredible compatibility for 3d assets, and great 2d and 3d compatibility. And not only that, all my past games were made exclusively in Unity, some using the 2d, some using the 3d engine. Now, for the final one it’s hard to choose from the multitude of genres, while also serving as a legacy, mine. Which is why I choose the 3d URP.<br>
SEE-ME-AGAIN is a final standalone work and an archival home for seven preceding projects. It was created not to replace what came before, but to give those works a place to rest together. Across eight entry points, this project functions as a reflective memoir: a chance to revisit past systems, refined mechanics, and earlier iterations from a final, unified perspective: my perspective upon the subjects they follow.<br>
And so, the question arises: what even is SEE-ME-AGAIN?<br>
Each remaster retains its core identity while fitting into the overall sequence.<br>
The Final Piece, aka SEE-ME-AGAIN, acts as the frame through which the whole journey can be viewed one last time. I’ll leave that idea towards the end.<br>
After years of waiting, nothing came. As your life flashed before your eyes you realize we’re looking in the wrong place. Searching for an ending you can’t even fathom.

# About
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Finishing TurboGrafx: BITROOT felt like reaching the top of a peak I had been climbing <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;for a very long time. It was the project I poured everything into. The programming, <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;the concept, the handmade art. When something takes that much out of you, moving <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;on to whatever comes next isn't simple. You don't just turn a page and forget what it <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;took to build it.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;SEE-ME-AGAIN isn't an attempt to outdo BITROOT, nor any other from my past. It exists <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;because I needed a place where I could try again. Do better. Start anew.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Across these seven remasters and this final standalone work, you’re seeing the <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;complete path, the rough edges, the choices that I had to take, and the recurring ideas <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;that eventually grew into my past projects and brought me here.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This is my memoir in code. A way to preserve the journey, look back one last time, and <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;officially close this chapter.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Thank you for playing, for listening, and for seeing it through to the end with me.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;To my friends, thank you. To my fans, farewell. To my haters.. Congratulations!<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;— Tudor
